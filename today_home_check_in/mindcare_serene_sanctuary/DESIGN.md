## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a sanctuary for the senses, explicitly tailored to reduce cognitive strain, visual overstimulation, and somatic anxiety. Designed for individuals seeking grounded mental wellness, mindful daily tracking, and therapeutic balance, the interface functions as a digital decompression chamber.

The core aesthetic blends **Organic Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Underpinnings**:
- **Breathe over Density**: Surfaces rely on generous, unhurried negative space to provide psychological relief and de-escalate urgency.
- **Physical Softness**: Forms simulate the gentle weight of linen paper, smoothed river stones, and matte ceramics. There are no harsh dividers, sharp corners, or jarring drop shadows.
- **Micro-Delight with Restraint**: Interactions favor fluid, dampened transitions that evoke mindful pacing rather than impulsive engagement.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in grounding botanicals and soft mineral pigments, specifically calibrated to eliminate optical vibration and bright white glare.

- **Primary (`#5B7E6B` - Deep Sage)**: The primary anchor for key actions, selected states, and pivotal navigation cues. Carries organic authority without clinical sterility.
- **Secondary (`#8FA998` - Soft Eucalyptus)**: A muted, restorative green used for secondary accents, progress arcs, and supportive illustrations.
- **Tertiary (`#9B8EB9` - Gentle Lavender)**: A contemplative, tranquil purple reserved for reflective moments, sleep routines, meditation flags, and emotional milestones.
- **Neutral (`#505A54` - Tranquil Slate)**: A deep, warm-tinted slate for body copy and icons, avoiding the harsh contrast of pure black against soft backgrounds.
- **Background Foundation**: Never pure white (`#FFFFFF`). All primary canvases rest on an organic, warm linen cream (`#FBF9F5`), while card surfaces utilize an airy off-white tint (`#F4F1EA` to `#FFFFFF` with low opacity) to cultivate natural hierarchy.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes open letterforms, humanistic terminals, and gentle geometry. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a warm, contemporary rhythm with friendly counters that reduce reading fatigue during moments of high stress.

- **Weight Discipline**: Ultra-bold weights (700+) are avoided. Semibold (600) is the maximum weight deployed across all headlines, ensuring structure without visual aggression.
- **Vertical Airflow**: Line heights are intentionally generous (+140% to +155% of font size) to allow effortless line tracking, preventing crowded paragraphs and visual tension.
- **Label Tuning**: Small uppercase formats are sparingly used and set with slight positive letter spacing to maintain clarity on mobile viewport scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine enforces breathing room as a functional wellness tool.

- **Layout Model**: A fluid 4-column layout on mobile, scaling to 8 columns on tablet, and 12 columns on desktop. On mobile devices, views maintain an intentional minimum horizontal margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`) to shield content from edge clipping and thumb occlusion.
- **Rhythm**: Layouts follow an 8pt base grid with an inner 4pt sub-grid for tight component alignments. Vertical section spacing is deliberately wider than standard productivity tools, leaning on `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to delineate topical shifts without heavy rules or borders.
- **Form-Factor Adaptations**:
  - *Mobile (<600px)*: Single-column flows with comfortable edge clearances; thumb-zone-first placement for all reflection inputs and audio controllers.
  - *Tablet & Desktop (≥600px)*: Maximum reading widths capped at `680px` for text and journaling views, maintaining an intimate personal sanctuary scale even on large screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built via soft environmental tonal layering and subtle, diffused illumination rather than dramatic drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is primarily communicated through surface pigmentation:
  - Base canvas: Warm linen cream (`#FBF9F5`).
  - Rested card level: Pale porcelain matte (`#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity or `#F6F4ED`).
  - Elevated modal/tray level: Soft tinted ivory (`#FFFFFF` at 95% opacity).
- **Ambient Chromatic Shadows**: When elevation is needed for floating interactive elements (e.g., bottom navigation bars, audio players), shadows utilize an ultra-diffused, botanical-tinted plume:
  - Resting elevation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(80, 90, 84, 0.04)`
  - Active/lifted elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(91, 126, 107, 0.08)`
- **Atmospheric Boundaries**: Borders are minimal, utilizing semi-translucent warm tones (`rgba(80, 90, 84, 0.06)`) resembling deckled paper edges rather than structural dividing lines.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry. Sharp corners create subconscious visual tension; rounded silhouettes provide a softer, calming tactile language.

- **Buttons & Chips**: Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide inviting touch targets that feel pebble-smooth and approachable.
- **Cards & Sheets**: Set to `rounded-xl` (`2rem` to `3rem` on larger elements) to give panels the gentle drape of organic surfaces.
- **Interactive Controls**: Sliders, progress bars, and track meters use fully rounded end caps to evoke flow and continuity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill-shaped containers finished in deep sage (`#5B7E6B`) with off-white text (`#FBF9F5`). Minimum height of `48px` to ensure effortless tap access without precision pressure. Pressed states shift slightly to a deeper botanical tone with gentle spring physics.
- **Secondary**: Clear background with a delicate border (`1.5px` solid `#8FA998` at 40% opacity) and neutral slate text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, displaying gentle sage or slate text with a soft hover/active pill background tint (`rgba(91, 126, 107, 0.08)`).

### Chips & Pill Badges
- Used for mood states, tag categorization, and session lengths.
- Unselected chips feature a semi-translucent linen base with soft slate labels.
- Selected chips shift to a muted eucalyptus (`#8FA998`) or gentle lavender (`#9B8EB9`) fill with tonal text. Badges never use alarming saturated colors.

### Cards
- Serene surfaces with generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Flat or micro-elevated with botanical ambient tint.
- Edge borders are either absent or kept to a soft `1px` translucent tone (`rgba(80, 90, 84, 0.06)`).

### Tactile Toggles & Switches
- Calming, organic toggles featuring an oblong pill track in pale sage/cream.
- The sliding thumb mimics an unglazed ceramic pebble with a micro ambient shadow. The transition is damped and rhythmic, avoiding snap animations.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Circular checkboxes replacing mechanical square boxes to preserve shape harmony.
- Selected state fills with primary sage and reveals a soft cream check mark with an organic ease-in scale transition.

### Input Fields
- Generously padded (`16px 20px`), pill-rounded or deeply rounded (`rounded-lg`) fields on subtle cream fills (`#F3EFE6`).
- Focus rings replace high-contrast outlines with a soft, diffused eucalyptus aura (`0 0 0 3px rgba(143, 169, 152, 0.35)`).

### Wellness-Specific Components
- **Breathing Metronome**: Concentric, pulsating pill and circular rings rendered in translucent lavender and sage, breathing outward and inward at guided therapeutic frequencies.
- **Mood Slider**: An uninterrupted tactile track with an oversized ceramic thumb that softly alters background tint saturation as the user moves from low to high valence.
- **Audio Scrubber Bar**: Thick, organic capsule track allowing seamless, thumb-friendly scrubbing through soundscapes and guided sessions.
