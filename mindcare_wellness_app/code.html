Tip of the Day

5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding

Whenever panic or racing thoughts surge, engage all senses sequentially to return your mind to physical safety:

Notice small details: the texture of wood grain on your desk, sunlight on a leaf, or shadows on the floor.
Feel your feet on the rug, the fabric against your arms, cool air on your nose, or the smooth ceramic of a mug.
Listen for passing cars, distant birds, the hum of a refrigerator, or gentle wind.
Breathe in fresh coffee, essential lavender oil, or simply fresh outdoor breeze.
Take a slow sip of herbal tea, cool water, or focus on the clean freshness of your breath.
